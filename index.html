why it's really anonymous

Not a setting. The schema.

  • ✦A message is stored against the receiver — there's no sender account attached to steal or leak.
  • ✦Database rules let only you read your own inbox. Nobody else can query it.
  • ✦We never record a sender name, email or profile. There is nothing to reveal.
